# Suzuki Raider R150 FI Master Workshop Service Menu

> **Standard Operating Requirement:** All servicing and overhauls listed in this document must strictly utilize **Suzuki Genuine Parts (SGP)** and **Suzuki Ecstar** factory-approved lubricants/fluids[cite: 23]. Aftermarket parts or non-spec fluids are strictly prohibited to preserve warranty compliance, engine longevity, and precision DOHC tolerances[cite: 23].

## 1. Preventive Maintenance Services (PMS)

### Service: Engine Oil & Filter Service
* **Materials Needed:** 
  * 1.3 Liters Suzuki Ecstar R9000 10W-40 Full Synthetic 4T Engine Oil[cite: 23]
  * 1x SGP Oil Filter (`16510-45H10-000`)[cite: 23]
  * 1x SGP Oil Filter Cap O-Ring (`09280-54001-000`)[cite: 23]
  * 1x SGP Inner Pipe O-Ring (`09280-13010-000`)[cite: 23]
  * 1x SGP Drain Plug Washer (`09168-10L02-000`)[cite: 23]
* **Purpose:** Maintains vital engine lubrication, reduces friction across the DOHC valvetrain, and flushes internal engine contaminants[cite: 23].

### Service: Spark Plug Replacement
* **Materials Needed:** 
  * 1x SGP NGK MR8E-9 Spark Plug (`09482-00646-000`)[cite: 23]
* **Purpose:** Guarantees crisp air-fuel ignition, prevents cylinder misfires, and maintains peak combustion efficiency[cite: 23].

### Service: Air Cleaner Element Service
* **Materials Needed:** 
  * 1x SGP Air Cleaner Filter Assembly (`13780-12K00-000`)[cite: 23]
  * 1x SGP Air Cleaner Box Gasket (`13746-12K00-000`)[cite: 23]
* **Purpose:** Ensures clean, unrestricted air intake into the throttle body and shields the cylinder bore from abrasive road dust[cite: 23].

### Service: Drive Chain & Sprocket Replacement
* **Materials Needed:** 
  * 1x SGP Chain & Sprocket Kit (14T/38T/116L - `27609-12800-000`) **OR** individual components[cite: 23]:
    * 1x SGP Drive Chain RK 428KLO (`27610-12K00-116`)[cite: 23]
    * 1x SGP Engine Front Sprocket 14T (`27511-25G10-000`)[cite: 23]
    * 1x SGP Rear Sprocket 38T (`64511-12K00-000`)[cite: 23]
  * 1x SGP Drive Chain Buffer Slider (`61273-25G10-000`)[cite: 23]
* **Purpose:** Restores direct power delivery from the transmission output shaft to the rear wheel while eliminating driveline slop and chain noise[cite: 23].

### Service: Radiator Coolant Flush & Refill
* **Materials Needed:** 
  * 1.0 Liter Suzuki Ecstar Long Life Coolant[cite: 23]
  * 1x SGP Water Bypass Hose Clip (`09401-10417-000`)[cite: 23]
* **Purpose:** Prevents thermal engine spikes, eliminates internal water jacket scale/corrosion, and stabilizes liquid-cooling efficiency[cite: 23].
